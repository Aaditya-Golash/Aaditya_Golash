## Problem
Organic social growth is noisy. Good content ideas fail without fast iteration, clear hooks, consistent production, and feedback loops that turn audience response into better decisions.

## Context
Across freelance and collaborative projects, I worked on short-form video channels where the goal was to grow attention without paid distribution.

## What I Built
I co-founded and grew multiple digital brands that generated **4M+ organic views**, including `@bof_tm` with **5K+ followers** and **2M+ views**. I also built the A2 TikTok channel with collaborators and launched the Phi Delta Theta UBCO TikTok from scratch.

## Key Decisions
- Used daily content production and rapid iteration to test hooks and formats.
- Applied trend analysis to identify content patterns before they peaked.
- Used audience analytics to improve reach, retention, and engagement.
- Treated content as a feedback system, not a one-off creative output.

## Tradeoffs
- Prioritized fast learning cycles over highly polished production in early testing.
- Focused on organic signal rather than paid boosts so performance data stayed cleaner.
- Balanced brand consistency with format experimentation.

## Impact
- Generated **4M+ organic views** across multiple digital brands.
- Helped grow A2 to **2M+ organic views** within one year.
- Grew the Phi Delta Theta UBCO TikTok from zero to **10K+ views** and increased campus engagement.
- Built stronger intuition for attention, feedback loops, and audience behavior.
