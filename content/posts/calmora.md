## Problem
Local service marketplaces face a cold-start problem: clients need provider coverage, providers need demand signal, and early teams need validation before building a full marketplace stack.

## Context
Calmora explored an "UberEats for services" concept connecting clients with local service providers. The goal was to validate demand and matching workflows before overbuilding the product.

## What I Built
I co-founded and developed a Wizard-of-Oz MVP with a website, scalable data structures, Python automation scripts for scraping and cleaning provider data, and Discord-based command control and logging for outreach workflows.

## Key Decisions
- Used a Wizard-of-Oz approach to validate demand before automating every marketplace interaction.
- Built Python scripts for scraping, cleaning, and outreach so early operations could move faster.
- Used Reddit research and cold outreach to compare demand signals across Vancouver and Calgary.
- Designed data workflows around reliable client-provider matching instead of just lead volume.

## Tradeoffs
- Chose lightweight validation over a polished marketplace product in the first phase.
- Kept automation operator-controlled through Discord to avoid brittle fully automated workflows.
- Focused on market signal quality before expanding provider categories.

## Impact
- Identified stronger early demand signals in Vancouver and Calgary.
- Built reusable data and outreach workflows for local-service marketplace testing.
- Created an MVP path that reduced product risk before deeper engineering investment.
