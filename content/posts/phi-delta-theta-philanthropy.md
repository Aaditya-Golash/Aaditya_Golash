## Problem
Community fundraising initiatives fail when ownership, logistics, partner communication, and follow-through are unclear. The work requires practical operations: aligning people, timelines, outreach, and execution details under volunteer constraints.

## Context
As Head of Philanthropy for Phi Delta Theta, I managed community-facing initiatives where the goal was to mobilize members, coordinate outreach, and convert planning into reliable event execution.

## What I Built
I led philanthropy planning and operating workflows, coordinated fundraising initiatives, organized member responsibilities, and managed communication across internal teams and community stakeholders.

## Key Decisions
- Treated philanthropy as an operations system with owners, timelines, outreach targets, and post-event follow-up.
- Broke initiatives into clear execution lanes: planning, promotion, logistics, volunteer staffing, and partner communication.
- Focused on repeatable event structure so future teams could reuse the workflow instead of rebuilding from scratch.
- Kept messaging direct and measurable, tying community outreach to concrete fundraising and participation goals.

## Tradeoffs
- Prioritized reliable execution and turnout over overly complex event formats.
- Used lightweight coordination tools because volunteer teams needed clarity more than heavy process.
- Focused on community trust and follow-through rather than one-off visibility.

## Impact
- Led philanthropy and community outreach operations for a volunteer organization.
- Coordinated fundraising initiatives from planning through execution and follow-up.
- Mobilized members around defined responsibilities, timelines, and stakeholder communication.
- Built operational leadership experience outside purely academic or technical settings.
